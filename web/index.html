01 · Install

Four steps, about a minute

Backrow isn't in the Chrome Web Store, so you add it in Developer mode. Everyone watching needs it, plus their own login for the streaming site.

  1. 1

    Download the zip

    Grab backrow-extension.zip. It's small and has no trackers or remote code.

    Download zip
  2. 2

    Unzip it

    Double-click the file. Keep the backrow-extension folder somewhere it won't get deleted, since Chrome loads it from there.

  3. 3

    Turn on Developer mode

    Paste this into the address bar, then flip the Developer mode switch in the top right.

  4. 4

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Pin Backrow from the puzzle-piece menu so it's easy to reach.

Then

Open a show and press play. Click the Backrow icon, choose Start party and send the invite link. Friends who open it land on the same episode, at the same second.

02 · Your own video

Stream a file from your computer

Nothing is uploaded. Your browser plays the file and streams it straight to each friend, compressed live in high quality and encrypted end to end. Friends watch in any browser, phones included, with no extension. Keep the tab open while you watch. Works best for small groups. Hosting needs Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

03 · Privacy

The server can't read your party

Every invite link ends in a secret key, the part after the #. Browsers never send that part to any server. Your messages, names and what you're watching are locked with it before they leave your browser, so Backrow's server only passes along scrambled data.

04 · Questions

Good to know

Is it actually free?

Yes. No ads or paid tier. Accounts are optional and only keep your profile on every device. It runs on Cloudflare's free plan, and the code is MIT licensed, so you can host your own copy.

Does everyone need a subscription?

For Netflix, Hulu, Peacock, Prime Video, HBO Max and Disney+, yes. Each person streams from their own account, and Backrow only keeps the players in sync. YouTube and uploaded videos need no account.

Do I need an account?

No. An account only saves your photo, name and color so they follow you to other browsers. They're encrypted with a key made from your password before they leave your browser, so the server can't read them. If you forget your password, resetting it clears that saved profile, since nothing else can unlock it.

Why isn't it in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. Loading it in Developer mode takes a minute and runs the same code you can read on GitHub.

Firefox or Safari?

The extension is Chromium-only for now. Friends can watch a video streamed from your computer in any modern browser, phones included.

Where does my own video go?

Nowhere but your friends' screens. It streams directly from your browser to theirs over an encrypted connection, and it's never uploaded or stored.

How tight is the sync?

Pauses and seeks reach everyone in well under a second. If someone drifts a little, Backrow speeds them up or slows them down slightly until they catch up, instead of jumping.
